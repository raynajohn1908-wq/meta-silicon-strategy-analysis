# Primary Source Ledger

This project prioritizes Meta's own investor relations and newsroom disclosures. The analysis does not treat secondary reporting as the source of record for the core claims below.

## Financial results

### Meta Platforms Q2 2026 Results

**Published:** July 29, 2026  
**Source:** Meta Investor Relations  
**Used for:**

- Q2 2026 revenue of $60.801B versus $47.516B in Q2 2025
- Q2 2026 costs and expenses of $42.026B
- Q2 2026 operating income of $18.775B versus $20.441B in Q2 2025
- Q2 2026 net income of $15.848B versus $18.337B in Q2 2025
- Q2 2026 free cash flow of $0.784B versus $8.549B in Q2 2025
- Q2 2026 capital expenditures of $31.08B
- FY2026 capex guidance of $130B to $145B
- FY2026 total expense guidance of $165B to $169B
- approximately 8,000 employees impacted by the May 2026 headcount reduction
- $1.18B of Q2 severance expenses associated with that reduction

## MTIA strategy

### Expanding Meta's Custom Silicon to Power Our AI Workloads

**Published:** March 11, 2026  
**Source:** Meta Newsroom  
**Used for:**

- four new MTIA generations within two years
- MTIA 300 in production for ranking and recommendation training
- MTIA 400, 450, and 500 capable of handling all workloads
- near-term emphasis on GenAI inference production
- hundreds of thousands of MTIA chips deployed for inference
- greater compute efficiency than general-use chips for intended workloads
- inference-first development strategy
- frictionless adoption through industry standards
- modularity intended to accelerate time to production

## Analytical boundary

The public sources above do not disclose a like-for-like MTIA versus external accelerator TCO series, a public accelerator-specific capex split, or quantified engineering migration cost across hardware. The project therefore does not estimate those values.
