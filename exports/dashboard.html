Is custom silicon actually solving Meta's AI infrastructure cost problem?

Meta raised 2026 capex guidance to $125B–$145B while free cash flow fell ~91% YoY in Q2 2026 — and in the same window, accelerated its custom-silicon program (MTIA) and signed a new long-term GPU deal with AMD. This dashboard visualizes that sequence using only Meta's own disclosed figures.

MTIA chip generation roadmap

Four chip generations announced in two years — a fast cadence for the industry. Only MTIA 300 is in production; it trains ranking/recommendation models, not frontier LLMs. MTIA 400/450/500 are inference-only, primarily for GenAI workloads.

What Meta has not disclosed

Named explicitly rather than estimated — these are the specific gaps that keep this analysis from reaching a firm conclusion.

  • No cost-per-query or TCO figure comparing MTIA inference to GPU-based inference.
  • No capex breakdown by GPU spend vs. MTIA/custom-silicon spend within the $125B–$145B FY2026 guidance.
  • No disclosed timeline for whether custom-silicon investment is expected to reduce total infrastructure capex, versus simply changing its composition.
